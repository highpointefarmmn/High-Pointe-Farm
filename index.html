A Farm Built Around Exceptional Care

High Pointe Farm is a 22-stall full-care boarding facility serving eventing, dressage, and hunter/jumper riders who care deeply about their horses and their enjoyment of the sport.

We combine attentive daily horse care with a supportive, welcoming barn environment where competitive junior riders, adult amateurs, and those just beginning their riding journey feel equally at home.

High Pointe Farm is also passionate about helping the next generation of riders discover the joy of horses and develop a lifelong connection to the sport.
