A Farm Built Around Exceptional Care

High Pointe Farm is a 22-stall full-care boarding facility serving eventing, dressage, and hunter/jumper riders who care deeply about their horses and the experience of riding.

Located in Maple Plain, Minnesota, the farm offers a thoughtfully designed facility, dependable daily care, and a welcoming barn community where riders of all levels can feel comfortable and supported. We are especially passionate about introducing young riders to the sport and helping the next generation discover the joy of horses.

From competitive junior riders and adult amateurs to those new to horse ownership, High Pointe Farm is a place where horses receive exceptional care and riders enjoy spending time at the barn.

Exceptional Daily Horse Care

Horses at High Pointe Farm receive attentive, consistent daily care designed to support health, comfort, and performance. Our approach emphasizes routine, observation, and communication to ensure every horse thrives.

Daily Horse Care

  • Twice-daily grain feeding
  • Premium hay program
  • Supplements administered as provided by owners
  • Blanketing, fly masks, boots, and seasonal gear as needed
  • Daily turnout (weather permitting)

Wellness Oversight

  • Daily visual wellness checks
  • Injury monitoring and communication with owners
  • Coordination with veterinarian and farrier when needed

Ideal Boarders

High Pointe Farm is home to riders who value excellent horse care, a welcoming barn community, and the opportunity to enjoy time with their horses.

Competitive junior riders
Adult amateur riders
Riders new to horse ownership
Families introducing young riders to the sport

We welcome riders working with trainers in eventing, dressage, and hunter/jumper disciplines, as well as those who simply enjoy spending time in the saddle and developing their skills over time.

One of our passions at High Pointe Farm is introducing young riders to the world of horses and helping them build confidence, responsibility, and a lifelong love for the sport.

Flexible Training, Supportive Environment

Boarders are welcome to bring their own trainer, subject to prior farm approval.

Trainer Requirements

  • Receive approval from the farm
  • Provide proof of current liability insurance
  • Sign a High Pointe Farm Trainer Agreement
  • Adhere to all farm safety standards, including helmet policies

Preferred Connections

High Pointe Farm maintains relationships with respected professionals in eventing, dressage, and hunter/jumper disciplines. For boarders seeking training support, we are happy to provide introductions when requested.
